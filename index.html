HOW TO PLAY
GOAL
First player to reach 104 points wins the game.
SETUP
A coin flip decides who deals. The loser deals first, then dealer switches every round.

Dealer gives 4 cards to opponent, 4 to the table, 4 to themselves. Table cards stay until the round ends.
ON YOUR TURN
Select a card from your hand, then select table cards that sum to 11 together with your card. Hit CONFIRM to capture them — they go to your pile.

If you cannot sum to 11, press DROP CARD to place your card on the table.
SPECIAL CARDS
🃏 Jack — captures all table cards with value ≤ 11 (not Queen or King)
👸 Queen — can only capture another Queen
🤴 King — can only capture another King
SUHÈR BONUS
If you clear the last card(s) from the table (summing to 11, not with a Jack) and you have under 80 points — you earn +5 bonus points!
SCORING
Immediate points (earned when you capture):
• Each Ace → +1 pt
• Each Jack → +1 pt
• 2♣ Clubs → +2 pts
• 10♦ Diamonds → +3 pts

End of round (from your pile):
• Most clubs (7 or more out of 13) → +13 pts
ILLEGAL DROP
If you drop a card when a valid sum of 11 exists, the game automatically forces a random valid capture — so always check the table!
