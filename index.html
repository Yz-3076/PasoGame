PASO — RULES
GOAL
First to 104 points wins.
ROUND
One round = one full 52-card deck played to zero. When the deck runs out, round ends and points are counted.
DEALING
Coin flip — loser deals. Dealer picks: give opponent 4 cards first, OR give table 4 cards first. Then fills the other side. Then deals 4 to themselves. After that, only hands are refilled (4+4) — the table is never touched again until the round ends.
YOUR TURN
Pick a card from your hand + table cards that sum to 11 → CONFIRM to capture. Can't sum? → DROP to place your card on the table.
SPECIAL CARDS
J — captures all table cards value ≤ 11 (not Q or K)
Q — only captures another Q
K — only captures another K
SUHÈR
Clear the last table cards (not with Jack) under 80 pts → +5 bonus at round end!
SCORING (each round)
Most ♠ Spades (7+) → +13 pts
Each Ace → +1 pt · Each Jack → +1 pt
2♣ Clubs → +2 pts · 10♦ Diamonds → +3 pts · Max 26 pts/round
