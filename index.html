🃏 PASO — RULES
GOAL
First player to reach 104 points wins the game.
A ROUND
One round = one full 52-card deck. When the deck runs out, the round ends and points are counted.
DEALING
Coin flip — loser deals. Dealer chooses: give opponent 4 cards first, or table 4 cards first. Then fills the other. Then deals 4 to themselves. After that, only hands are refilled (4+4) when both run empty. The table is never touched again until the round ends.
YOUR TURN
Select a card from your hand + table cards that sum to 11 → CONFIRM to capture.
Can't sum to 11? → DROP to place your card on the table.
SPECIAL CARDS
♟ Jack — captures all table cards with value ≤ 11 (not Q or K)
👸 Queen — only captures another Queen
🤴 King — only captures another King
END OF ROUND
The last player to capture cards gets all remaining table cards. Then count points from your pile.
SUHÈR BONUS
Clear the last cards from the table (summing to 11, not with Jack) when you have under 80 points → +5 bonus!
SCORING (per round)
• Most ♠ Spades (7 or more) → +13 pts
• Each Ace → +1 pt
• Each Jack → +1 pt
• 2♣ Clubs → +2 pts
• 10♦ Diamonds → +3 pts
• Suhèr bonus → +5 pts
Max per round: 26 points
